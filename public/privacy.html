Privacy

Effective July 28, 2026. This notice covers the KIM3310 Systems website and its private inquiry form. Do not submit credentials, private datasets, health records, payment details, or other sensitive material.

Analytics

Google Analytics starts with Consent Mode denied. The footer consent control can enable aggregate analytics. Commerce CTA events, when enabled, use lane-level metadata only.

Private Inquiries

The form stores the inquiry type, your email, optional organization, selected service, optional source product or repository, budget or price-research range, message, consent version, creation time, and processing status in Cloudflare D1. For abuse prevention, it also stores a one-way email hash and a date-scoped, secret-salted one-way network fingerprint. The network address is transformed in memory and is not inserted into the inquiry database.

Inquiry records are scheduled for deletion 90 days after collection and are pruned by an independent daily retention job. Deletion can occur during the next daily run. A longer period is used only when an active engagement has a separately documented retention basis. Access is restricted to the site operator and is used only to evaluate and respond to the requested service.

Checkout Providers

Configured checkout links may open an independent payment provider. KIM3310 Systems does not collect card or bank details on this website. The provider's privacy and billing terms apply to its checkout.

Data Requests

Submit a new private inquiry with your original reference and state whether you request access, correction, or deletion. Identity must be verified through the same email address before a record is disclosed or changed.
